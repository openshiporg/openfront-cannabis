# Storefront (customer) surface brief

Scope: `/` (menu), `/menu/[id]`, `/visit`, the not-found pages and the age gate. Mode: Persuade on `/`; Operate on the menu filters, item and visit pages. Audience and job are in PRODUCT.md. Constraints: every route, state and workflow stays; in-store purchase only; age gate first; dark ground (owner, 2026-10-24); new mark and type pairing (owner, 2026-10-25).

Main tasks, in order: is it open and where is it; what is on the menu in the form I want, at what price; read one item; go.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The storefront is the store's own house catalog, printed like a 1990s seed-bank catalog: a black glossy cover with one real plant photograph, then the menu as chapters by form, each opened by a solid coloured chapter band and filled with spec-table entries. It refuses the neon-green dispensary template, the cream apothecary label, and the previous rolling-paper booklet (monogram tiles, streaked leaves).
OWN-WORLD: Warm catalog black (#0d0f0c) with a lifted page (#161a14); bone ink (#ece8dc) for text; one process ink per chapter (flower sap green, pre-roll amber, edible rose, concentrate resin gold, vape glass blue, topical lilac), used only for chapter bands, the chapter index rule and the item plate. Bricolage Grotesque, condensed and heavy, prints names, prices and chapter titles; Albert Sans sets everything read. Corners 4px or less; 1px rules in the catalog grid; no cards, no glow, no gradient.
STORY: The visitor confirms their age, sees the store name over the cover photo with open state, hours, address and directions beside it, picks a chapter from the index (or searches), scans name, form, channel and price down one aligned price column, opens an item's spec page, and walks in.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 7:5. Left, the cover photograph full height of the fold with the store name (up to 112px, condensed) and tagline set on its lower edge. Right, the visit block (open state, hours, address, Directions button, payment, age rule) above the chapter index: every form with its count as a large underlined link list. The search field and the first chapter band start just below the fold edge. Phone: photo 4:3 with the name, then visit block, then the sticky chapter rail.
FORM: Seed-bank catalog, number 3 on my ordered list (1 dispensary menu board behind the counter, 2 child-resistant exit bag with compliance sticker, 3 seed-bank catalog, 4 blacklight headshop poster, 5 deli-style strain jars with hand labels, 6 grow tent under purple LEDs, 7 herbarium sheet). seed:2c0cbe4c775bac1d. Raises: chapter bands stay sticky while their entries scroll and carry one message each (terminal yellow wayfinding, competitive); state colour is reserved for open, closed and caution, never for chapters (night six-pack); every chapter and filter prints its honest count (minihompy room); the spec tables share one ruled grid that never moves while names and photos land on it (passport); one governing alignment, the price column, runs through every chapter (curved-crease shell). Signature interaction: the chapter band you are reading stays pinned and the chapter index marks it, so the catalog always says which chapter you are in.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
