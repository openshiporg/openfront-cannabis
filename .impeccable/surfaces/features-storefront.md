# Storefront (customer) surface brief

Scope: `/` (menu), `/menu/[id]`, `/visit`, the not-found pages and the age gate. Mode: Persuade on `/`; Operate on the menu filters, item and visit pages. Audience and job are in PRODUCT.md. Constraints: every route, state and workflow stays; in-store purchase only; age gate first; the owner pinned a dark, premium aesthetic (2026-10-24 brief).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The storefront is the store's rolling-paper booklet, printed in one ink on resin. The booklet's cover (age, name, open state, where) stays still beside the menu, and every item is a thin translucent leaf. It refuses the cream "cure label" page (the previous look) and the neon-green dispensary template.
OWN-WORLD: A resin-black ground in the store's accent hue, with one smoke-paper ink (#ebe5d3) doing text, figures and solid button blocks (the chosen pill is an ink block with ground-coloured type). The cover is a deeper hue-tinted stock; leaves are translucent paper with a gummed fold along the top. Besley (Clarendon) prints names, ages and prices; Libre Franklin sets everything read. Corners are square, at 2px; no hairlines.
STORY: The visitor confirms their age, sees the age rule, the store's name, whether it's open and where it is on the cover, filters the menu by form, channel or price, opens an item's record, and walks in.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop splits 5:7. On the left, the sticky cover: the age (88px), the store name stamped up to 88px, the open state, tagline, description, address, hours and directions. On the right, Menu at 52px with its count, a search field, form, channel and sort tracks, and the first leaves. On a phone the cover comes first, then the menu.
FORM: Rolling-paper booklet, number 7 on my ordered list (1 apothecary strain jars, 2 certificate-of-analysis lab report, 3 compliance mylar pouch, 4 grow room under night LEDs, 5 menu board, 6 herbarium sheet, 7 rolling-paper booklet). seed:b52912225961c42a. Raises: one restricted ink set does everything (state kiosk printed goods); the field's legend governs every mark (orienteering map). Signature interaction: leaves deal in once and lift when pointed at, as if pulled from the booklet.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
