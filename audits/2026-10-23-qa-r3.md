## Major

### QA-035: New timestamp columns on model lists cause hydration errors and show times in the viewer's zone, with no label
- **Area:** dashboard
- **URLs:** /dashboard/inventory-packages, /audit-events, /cannabis-licenses, /staff-credentials, /storefront-settings, /jurisdiction-rule-packs, /regulated-manifests, /inventory-events, /register-shifts, /cash-events, /tender-configurations, /idempotency-keys, /outbox-events, /compliance-incidents
- **Steps:** Sign in from a browser outside UTC (mine is US Central) and open any of these lists.
- **Expected:** no console errors; times in the premises zone with a label, as the Platform pages do ("Oct 23, 2026, 3:57 PM MDT"); "usable through" shown as a date.
- **Actual:**
  - Each list throws "Minified React error #418" (hydration text mismatch) on every load. That's 14 lists, all of them added to `initialColumns` in the QA-030 fix.
  - Times are shown in the viewer's browser zone, with no zone label:
    - `QA-MAN-0001` "Occurred At" shows "Oct 23, 2026 4:57 PM" (Platform: 3:57 PM MDT).
    - `QA-PKG-0001` "Expires At" shows "Jul 1, 2027 1:00 AM", while Inventory says "Usable through Jun 30, 2027".
    - License "Expires At" shows "Oct 23, 2027 4:37 PM".
  - Money columns are raw cents: Cash Events "Amount Cents 20000", Register Shifts "Variance Cents".
- **Likely files:** `features/keystone/models/index.ts` (the `listView.initialColumns` added in `4e5ef76`). The timestamp cell is in the shared, parity-locked `features/dashboard` field views. Either drop the timestamp and cents columns from `initialColumns` (the Platform pages already show these values correctly), or fix the shared cell upstream.
